About Us

PREDATORS FC is the united football identity of CGHS-18, born from the merger of two teams — CGHS HAWKEYE and CGHS MEGALODONS.

Built on discipline, intensity, teamwork, and competitive spirit, PREDATORS FC represents more than just a team. It is a symbol of unity, ambition, and the relentless mindset of players who refuse to back down. By combining the precision and vision of the Hawkeye with the dominance and strength of the Megalodons, the team was created to form a new standard of excellence both on and off the field.

At PREDATORS FC, we believe football is about brotherhood, resilience, and representing our community with pride. Every match is played with passion, strategy, and the hunger to win — the true mentality of predators.

From local competitions to future championships, our mission is to build a legacy that inspires confidence and respect in every opponent we face.

One Team. One Identity.
